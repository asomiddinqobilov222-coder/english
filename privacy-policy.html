Privacy Policy

English So'z · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how English So'z ("the app") handles information when you use the English vocabulary and learning application available at https://asomiddinqobilov222-coder.github.io/english/.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, the authentication service may provide your email address, account identifier, and basic profile information such as your name or profile picture.
  • Learning data: Words you save, examples, learning progress, review history, and app preferences may be stored to provide the app's features.
  • Device storage: The app may store a local backup, preferences, and settings in your browser's local storage. If you enter an optional AI provider API key, the current app may store that key in browser local storage on that device. Do not enter a key on a shared or untrusted device.
  • Support messages: If you contact the developer, the information you include in your message may be used to respond.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, save and synchronize your vocabulary and learning progress, restore your learning data, operate app settings, and provide features you choose to use. We do not sell personal information.

3. Services provided by other companies

The app uses Google sign-in through Supabase authentication and uses a Supabase database to store account-associated learning data. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy

If you use dictionary, translation, or optional AI features, text you submit for those features may be sent to the relevant third-party service to produce a result. Those services may process the submitted text under their own policies. Do not submit sensitive personal information to these features.

4. Storage and security

Account-associated learning data is stored using Supabase. Some settings or backups may remain in your browser on your device. We use the security features provided by the services integrated into the app, but no method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your Google account and any API keys secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Learning data may remain in your account database until it is deleted by the app's supported controls or a deletion request is handled. Signing out does not necessarily delete saved account data or browser-local data. To ask about access to or deletion of account-associated data, contact the developer using the project page below. Do not post private account details or secrets in a public issue.

6. Children's privacy

English So'z is a general language-learning app and is not intended to knowingly collect more personal information than is needed to provide its features. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact the developer.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the updated date shown near the top.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, visit the English So'z project page: https://github.com/asomiddinqobilov222-coder/english. Please avoid sharing passwords, API keys, or other sensitive information in public messages.

This policy describes the app's current intended data handling and should be reviewed whenever the app's providers or data practices change.
